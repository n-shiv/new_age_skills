# Adaptability & Resilience — Introduction

## 1. Core Idea
- **Adaptability:** Adjusting your approach to suit changing situations.
- **Resilience:** Staying steady, recovering, and moving forward during difficult times.
- **Psychological:** Related to the mind, thoughts, and emotions.

**Key takeaway:** Being future-ready means preparing for change—not just reacting when it happens.

## 2. Why These Skills Matter
- Work is constantly changing → new challenges, uncertainty, and pressure.
- Adaptability helps you **learn quickly** and **apply learning in different situations**.
- Resilience helps you **manage setbacks** and keep going.
- Benefits: lower stress, better relationships, sounder decisions, more rewarding work.
- Adaptability is **not a fixed personality trait** → it can be developed through practice.

## 3. What Adaptability Looks Like at Work

| Workplace situation | Less helpful response | More adaptable response |
|---|---|---|
| New role / pressure to be the expert | Feel you must always have the answer; push your own view | Stay open; ask questions; learn with the team |
| Difficult working relationship | Avoid the person or keep things formal | Build a genuine connection; share and listen |
| Heavy workload / burnout risk | Keep working without recovery | Set boundaries; make time to recharge |
| Disagreement / conflict | Avoid speaking up; accept unclear priorities | Raise concerns; challenge assumptions respectfully; align on priorities |
| Loss of motivation | Focus only on tasks | Reconnect work to personal and team purpose |

## 4. Three Drivers of Adaptability & Resilience

### 1. Set Learning Intentions & Build Positive Habits
- Be clear about **what you want to learn**.
- Use effective learning behaviours.
- Repeat behaviours until they become habits.
- Use the **Habit Loop** to build habits.

### 2. Develop Awareness of Mindsets & Well-being
- Notice your thoughts, emotions, and reactions—especially under pressure.
- Recognize mindsets that help or block progress.
- Choose more purposeful, productive responses.
- Look after your well-being to handle change effectively.

### 3. Connect Actions to Personal & Organizational Purpose
- Understand **why your work matters**.
- Link daily tasks to meaningful outcomes.
- Connect personal values with team and organizational goals.
- Purpose strengthens motivation during difficult periods.

## 5. Tools & Learning Outcomes
- **Learning intentions:** Set a clear goal when learning something new.
- **Effective learner behaviours:** Practise actions that make learning more effective.
- **Habit Loop:** Turn useful behaviours into repeatable habits.
- **APR framework:** Approach challenging situations with an adaptable and resilient mindset.

## Quick Takeaways
- Adaptability and resilience are **developable skills**, not fixed traits.
- Prepare for change; don't wait for pressure to build.
- Learn intentionally, build positive habits, and stay aware of your mindset.
- Protect your energy and maintain meaningful relationships.
- Let purpose guide your effort when work becomes challenging.

**Remember:** Adaptability helps you adjust; resilience helps you keep moving. Both grow through consistent practice.
